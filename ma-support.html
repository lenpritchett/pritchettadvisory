M&A Support

Due diligence, synergy modeling, and integration planning

Successful M&A requires more than financial modeling—it demands operational insight, intentional communication, integration expertise, and disciplined execution. We help private equity firms and corporate acquirers maximize value creation through the entire deal lifecycle.

Commercial & Operational Due Diligence

Deep assessment of commercial strategies, operational capabilities, and value creation opportunities.

  • • Market and competitive positioning analysis
  • • Revenue quality and growth assessment
  • • Operational efficiency evaluation
  • • Management capability assessment

Rigorous analysis of revenue and cost synergies with realistic implementation roadmaps.

  • • Cost reduction and efficiency opportunities
  • • Implementation sequencing and timing
  • • Risk assessment and mitigation

Integration Planning & Execution

Hands-on leadership to drive seamless integration and accelerate value capture.

  • • Day 1 readiness planning
  • • Integration management office (IMO) leadership
  • • Change management and communication
